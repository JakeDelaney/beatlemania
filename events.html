Nighthawks - Beatles Tribute!

  • 14/04/2025
  • 8:00 - 9.30 PM
  • 10 Wellington Quay, Temple Bar, Dublin, D02 VX36

Join us for Nighthawks, a special evening dedicated to the timeless magic of The Beatles! Celebrate the legendary band with a live tribute performance that will transport you back to the swinging '60s, along with a hilarious comedian to keep the laughs rolling.

The night also features an exciting play that brings the spirit of the Fab Four to life in a whole new way. Whether you're a lifelong Beatles fan or discovering their music for the first time, Nighthawks promises an unforgettable experience full of music, humor, and nostalgia!

Let it Beatles at the lighthouse!

  • 20/06/2025
  • 10:00 - 12:00 PM
  • 88 George's Street Lower, Dún Laoghaire, Dublin, A96 YA39

Get ready for an unforgettable night of classic hits as Let it Beatles, one of the best tribute bands around, takes the stage at the Lighthouse in Dun Laoghaire! Experience the magic of The Beatles' timeless music, performed with incredible accuracy and passion.
